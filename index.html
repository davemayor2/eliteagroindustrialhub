HOW THE MODEL WORKS

Four structured steps from land acquisition to annual harvest

Elite Palm Field combines real estate security with commercial palm cultivation. Every subscriber owns a documented parcel, the trees on it, and the harvested produce.

Select your preferred allocation — 2 Plots, Half Acre, or 1+ Acres — with outright or flexible payment plans. Upon subscription, you receive your Contract of Sale, Payment Receipt, and Acknowledgement Letter.

Virtual Allocation: Issued at the end of each quarter via email with a pre-allocation letter showing your plot on the master layout. Physical Allocation: Follows land preparation, with hard copies of your Registered Survey and Deed of Assignment (plus estate C of O perfection in progress).

Elite Agro Industrial Hub handles land clearing, seasonal May/June planting of certified F1 Tenera seedlings (with over 1,000 palms already on ground), fertilization cycles, irrigation, pest management, and NIFOR-aligned agronomy.

Early fruiting begins in Year 3–4, reaching peak output from Year 5 onward and continuing for 25–30 years. Output is measured in metric tonnes: 80% goes to the subscriber (collect directly or monetize through our estate processing system) and 20% covers management.
